Privacy Policy


Effective date: 2025.12.25


This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Numer app. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the App.


1) Information we collect


Account information
- Email address and password (handled by Firebase Authentication).
- Display name.


Profile information (optional)
- Profile name, major, contact info, personal goal.
- Avatar data (selected avatar index/color or a photo you choose from your device).


Study and activity data
- Courses/subjects you create.
- Study sessions (timestamps, duration, course association, and session type such as timer/stopwatch/pomodoro).
- Reflections or notes you enter after sessions.
- Goals and progress data.


Group and community data
- Group details (name, description, visibility).
- Membership, roles, join timestamps, and group activity/leaderboard stats.
- Join codes for private groups (stored by group administrators).


Device and app data
- A locally generated device identifier used for sync and account safety.
- App settings (theme, locale, notification preferences).
- Local notification schedules/alarms stored on your device.


2) How we use information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Provide core features like courses, timers, sessions, goals, and reflections.
- Sync your data across devices.
- Enable group functionality, leaderboards, and aggregated study statistics.
- Communicate important account or verification information.
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the App.


3) AI analysis
AI analysis is currently unavailable. The current version of the App does not send your course names, study session summaries, or reflection text to an AI service.


4) Sharing and disclosure
We share information in the following cases:
- Service providers: We use Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check) and Google Cloud services to host and process data.
- Group visibility: Your profile name, avatar, and study statistics may be visible to members of the groups you join, depending on group settings.
- Legal and safety: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users, the App, or our rights.


We do not sell your personal information.


5) Data retention
We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. You can delete your account from the App, which removes your profile and related data from our systems, subject to legal or operational requirements. Some data may persist in backups for a limited period.


6) Your choices and rights
- Access and update: You can update your profile information in the App.
- Delete account: You can delete your account from the App. This will remove your account data from Firebase and local storage.
- Notifications: You can disable local notifications in device settings.


7) Security
We use industry-standard security measures and rely on Firebase security features to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.


8) Children's privacy
The App is not intended for children under 8 (or the minimum age required by your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


9) International transfers
Your data may be processed and stored in countries where our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.


10) Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date and, if changes are material, provide additional notice in the App.
